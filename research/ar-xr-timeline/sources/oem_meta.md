## 1. Profile

Meta Platforms (Nasdaq: META) builds consumer XR hardware through its Reality Labs division: the Quest standalone-VR line (Quest 2/Pro/3/3S), camera-equipped "AI glasses" co-developed and co-manufactured with EssilorLuxottica (Ray-Ban Stories 2021 → Ray-Ban Meta 2023 → Oakley Meta and Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 2025 → Meta Glasses, Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 and camera-free Ray-Ban Meta Audio 2026), the monocular-HUD Meta Ray-Ban Display with the sEMG Neural Band (Sept 2025), and the Orion true-AR prototype (Sept 2024). Reality Labs lost US$19.19bn on US$2.21bn revenue in FY2025, and in 2025–26 Meta pivoted investment from headsets to glasses: Zuckerberg said glasses sales "more than tripled" in 2025, Omdia counts 7.4m Meta AI glasses shipped in 2025 and 3.4m in 1H26 (81% share). The device most relevant to autofocus suppliers is **Meta VR Glasses** (codename Phoenix/Loma/Puffin), announced 2026-09-23 for spring 2027 at US$1,299.99: a ~100 g micro-OLED passthrough headset tethered to a 300 g compute puck, whose passthrough Meta's own developer documentation specifies as "Color stereoscopic (26 PPD) passthrough with autofocus and depth sensing" — the first Meta consumer device whose world-facing cameras are documented as autofocus (every Ray-Ban/Oakley/Display camera to date is fixed-focus). Assembly is dominated by Goertek (Quest, Ray-Ban Meta, Hypernova/Display, and reportedly Phoenix), with EssilorLuxottica, Luxshare and (per press) Sunny Optical camera modules; no public source names the autofocus actuator or camera-module supplier for the 2027 VR Glasses.

## 4. Camera / autofocus relevance

**Meta VR Glasses (spring 2027) — the only Meta consumer device documented with autofocus world cameras.**
- Meta's developer "Compare devices" table: Meta VR Glasses passthrough = "Color stereoscopic (26 PPD) passthrough with autofocus and depth sensing"; Quest 3 = "Color stereoscopic 4 MP (18 PPD) passthrough with depth sensor"; Quest 3S = "Color stereoscopic 4 MP (18 PPD) passthrough" (https://developers.meta.com/vr/resources/compare-devices/). Developer landing page: "High-quality, full-color passthrough with autofocus and depth" (https://developers.meta.com/vr/discover/meta-vr-glasses/). Road to VR reproduces Meta's spec sheet with the rows "Passthrough Cameras: 26 PPD RGB / Autofocus: Yes / Depth Sensing: Yes" (https://roadtovr.com/meta-vr-glasses-unveiled-price-release-date/).
- Camera architecture per Road to VR/UploadVR: "eye-tracking cameras [4, 2 per eye] for UI selection and biometric unlock, front and downward-facing camera sensors for a wider hand-tracking range, and full color passthrough sensors offering 26 PPD", plus 6DoF tracking. Firmware visuals (Sept 2026) show sensors on the temple sides/undersides. **Not disclosed:** passthrough camera count (stereoscopic implies ≥2), sensor resolution/MP, FOV, actuator type, or module supplier. Hands-on reviewers (Road to VR, 2026-09-23) reported "seemingly zero weird bendiness of the environment or near-field objects" and "the best [passthrough] I've seen on a standalone VR headset".
- Why autofocus here: the device is marketed for virtual monitors, surface keyboard on desks, hologram calls and seated MR — near-field use where fixed-focus passthrough blurs hands/keyboards/phones. 26 PPD passthrough (vs 18 PPD Quest 3) also demands sharper optics.

**All Meta AI glasses to date are fixed-focus:** Ray-Ban Stories (dual 5MP; TechInsights: "fixed focus lens barrel, with no voice coil or magnets"), Ray-Ban Meta 2023 (12MP, fixed), Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 (12MP, 3K; PCMag "simple fixed focus"), Oakley Meta HSTN/Vanguard (12MP, 122° on Vanguard), Meta Ray-Ban Display (12MP, 3× digital zoom), Meta Glasses (12MP, 3K), Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 (12MP, 3K — focus type not stated; no review found claiming AF). Ray-Ban Meta Audio has no camera. Meta's own Reality Labs Research paper (arXiv 2504.13060, 2025-04-17) argues against autofocus for all-day AI glasses ("the actuator and control electronics add size and weight … and require more power"; always-on sensing "may need to be driven continuously"), favouring a 1 mm entrance-pupil fixed-focus design. This is a counter-signal for TLens in AI glasses, but not for the VR Glasses passthrough use case.

**Supplier clues:**
- Camera modules for Ray-Ban Meta / Display: Sunny Optical (ChinaTalk 2026-01-07; Tencent/OFweek 2026-09-20 also names Sony sensors). No teardown has identified the Display's camera module vendor (iFixit, Guttag silent on it).
- Phoenix/VR Glasses: Goertek assembly + pancake module, Visuotec micro-OLED, Qualcomm SoC (Tencent 2026-09-20, circumstantial). No source names the passthrough camera module or AF actuator supplier.
- Q Technology ↔ Meta: only a Xueqiu forum post (2026-09-22) asserting Q Tech supplies Quest tracking/passthrough modules; the user's prior research cites a 2024 Q Tech XR deck with a "US M brand" vendor code (not re-verified here). Treat as unconfirmed.
- Job postings: a current Reality Labs "Technical Lead, Camera IQ Assessment and Design" role covers lens/sensor selection and "passthrough video applications" but does not mention autofocus, tunable or liquid lenses; no Meta posting with "tunable lens"/"piezo" was found (Firecrawl/WebSearch, Oct 2026).
- LinkedIn: a profile (Lidu H., now at Tesla) states "Led development of miniature piezoelectric tunable lens AF camera module, exceeding design targets and passing full reliability qualifications for AR/VR/MR" (https://www.linkedin.com/in/lidu-h-3825903; search snippet only — employer/tenure could not be scraped here; the user's brief attributes it to a Meta reliability role Sept 2020–Apr 2025). Circumstantial.
- Patents: Meta's piezo-membrane tunable-lens AF filings (US 12,663,619 etc.) are the strongest Meta-side signal of interest in the TLens technology class; see patents agent.

## Return summary

Meta's cadence is an annual Connect (late Sept) with 0–34-day announce→ship lags for mature glasses/Quest lines; the exception is **Meta VR Glasses** (ex-Phoenix/Loma/Puffin), announced 2026-09-23 for "Spring 2027" at US$1,299.99 (~100 g, 300 g Snapdragon Reality Elite puck, 2412×2288 micro-OLED, 37 PPD). All Ray-Ban/Oakley/Display/Meta Glasses cameras (12MP) are fixed-focus, and a 2025 Meta Reality Labs paper argues against AF for all-day AI glasses. The ultralight headset's target moved 2027→2026→H1 2027→spring 2027. Supply chain: Goertek (Quest, Ray-Ban Meta, Hypernova, reportedly Phoenix), EssilorLuxottica (10m→20m capacity), Luxshare (Vietnam VR line), Sunny Optical camera modules; Q Tech↔Meta only forum-level. Artemis AR "2027" remains uncommitted (Bosworth, 2026-09-24); Quest 4 "Griffin" ≥H2 2027.
