## 1. Profile
Q Technology is a Kunshan-based camera-module and fingerprint ("biological recognition") module maker. It also makes LiDAR. Mr. He Ningning controls about 63% of it, partly through Q Technology Investment Inc. ("QT Investment", BVI). In H1 2026 revenue was RMB 9,923m (+12.4% YoY) and gross margin 6.6%. Four customers each supplied more than 10% of revenue. Since 2025-06-04 it has been poLight's largest shareholder: 32.97% at completion, 29.96% at 2025-12-31 and 29.90% at 2026-06-30. It has the right to appoint 2 of poLight's 7 directors. Its own decks list "VR VST TLens AF camera" and "AR SWIS TLens AF camera" as XR "Future Plan" items (2025-08). The 2026-08-24 deck says "TLens + AR Mono Engine — Volume Delivery Achieved" and "Overseas Key Customers — Ramp in 2H26 / 2027". The actuator side (Heyuan Youhua/"YOVA", Xiamen Zhonghui, and the ex-TDK business now called Actutek, with sites in Taiwan and the Philippines) sits in **Mr. He's private QT Investment Group, not in the listed company**.
