Compiled 2026-10-06 from simplified- and traditional-Chinese sources (company IR decks on qtechsmartvision.com and sunnyoptical.com, HKEX-mirrored announcements on cfi.net.cn, 腾讯新闻/搜狐/OFweek/映维网/VR陀螺/工商時報/UDN/LEDinside press, broker notes mirrored on 新浪财经/东方财富/finet, Citi via AAStocks). English translations are mine. Round-1 facts (BRIEF2) are not repeated unless a Chinese source adds wording or dates.

## 1. Profile

This file covers the Chinese-language evidence trail for three questions: (a) who supplies the cameras, VST/passthrough camera modules and autofocus actuators for Meta's 2027 "VR Glasses" (codename Phoenix, 凤凰); (b) what Q Technology (丘钛科技, HK:1478) and Sunny Optical (舜宇光学, HK:2382) say in Chinese about TLens/AF cameras for XR glasses, their overseas ("海外"/"美国M") customers and 2H26/2027 ramps; (c) what Chinese press and brokers say about Goertek (歌尔)/Luxshare (立讯) Phoenix assembly and about poLight. Q Tech is the world's no. 2–3 smartphone camera-module maker, poLight's largest shareholder since June 2025, and, through its controlling shareholder 丘钛投资, now owns TDK's former camera-actuator unit Actutek (艾特电). Sunny is the incumbent camera-module supplier to Meta's Quest Pro and Ray-Ban Meta lines per Chinese press and holds 33.33% of 歌尔光学. Goertek is the exclusive ODM of every Meta Quest headset per Chinese press. The headline result: Chinese sources name Goertek (assembly + Pancake), 视涯 Seeya (Micro-OLED) and Qualcomm for Phoenix, but no Chinese or Taiwanese source names the Phoenix passthrough camera-module or actuator supplier, and none links poLight/TLens to Meta's VR Glasses after the 2026-09-23 announcement.
