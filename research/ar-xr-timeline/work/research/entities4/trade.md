## 1. Profile

poLight's TLens supply chain has four stages:
- The PZT thin-film piezo MEMS wafers come from **STMicroelectronics' 8-inch (200 mm) fab in Agrate, Italy**. ST's TFP process was launched in 2014 with poLight as one of its first customers.
- The polymer is made at poLight's headquarters in Norway.
- Assembly and final test are done at **Tong Hsing Electronic Industries ("THEIL", transcribed "Tongxing" in poLight calls)**. This moved from Taiwan to the Philippines between 2020 and 2023, and Tong Hsing's Philippine subsidiary is in Calamba/Canlubang, Laguna.
- Finished TLens go to camera-module makers. In 2025–26 a second assembly-and-test line was added at Q Tech in China.

The Philippine plant that Q Tech's controlling shareholder acquired from TDK is a separate site. **Actutek Philippines Corporation** in Santo Tomas, Batangas makes voice-coil (VCM) and OIS camera-module actuators, not camera modules. TLens is tiny, high-value and air-freighted, and it flows inside Asia. As a result **no public bill-of-lading or customs source shows any TLens or poLight shipment**, and aggregate HS statistics are far too coarse to detect it.

The strongest public production-ramp signal for Meta VR Glasses ("Phoenix") is a display item, not a camera item. Per The Elec (2026-08-28), SeeYA has been mass-producing the OLEDoS panels since May 2026 and had shipped about 1 million panels, which is two per headset. No public source gives camera-module allocations or build volumes.

## 4. Camera / autofocus relevance

- **TLens flow, as poLight states it:** gel (Norway) → MEMS wafer (ST, Agrate 8-inch, Italy) → TLens assembly and final test (Tong Hsing Philippines; from 2026 also Q Tech, China) → "Module integrator" → "Assembly partner/OEM". That last pair is the wording on poLight's Q3 2025–Q2 2026 supply-chain slide.
- **Programme units for Program A:** the CEO's 2026-02-25 statement that these units are "likely coming from the Philippines" refers to **TLens lenses from the Tong Hsing line**. It does not mean camera modules from a Q Tech Philippine plant. Three things support this:
  - Q Tech's own footprint lists only an *actuator* plant in the Philippines (Actutek, a connected company, not a Q Tech subsidiary).
  - Q Tech's camera-module plants are in Kunshan, Vietnam and India.
  - The CEO said the units "will go into camera modules" after qualification.
- **Actutek Philippines:** Lot 2 Block 7, Light Industry & Science Park III SEZ, San Rafael, Santo Tomas, Batangas, the same park as "TDK Philippines Corporation Plant 3". PhilJobNet lists products as "Inductors, (CMA) including Voice Coil holders (VCH) and Optical Image Stabilizers (OIS)" and size as "200 and over (Large)". TDK Philippines Corp. (Laguna Technopark, Biñan) has assembled CMAs since 2014 (EMIS headcount 2,000–3,999 for the whole company). Everbright put the whole TDK actuator deal at "2000+ employees" (round-1 dataset). **No source shows camera-module assembly, TLens handling or XR work at Actutek Philippines.**
- **Meta VR Glasses:** Meta's developer page copy (saved by another round-4 agent at `entities4/dl_primary/meta_vr_glasses_live.txt`) reads "Open-periphery color passthrough with autofocus and depth sensing". No trade, customs, teardown or analyst source names the camera-module or AF-actuator vendor. The only module-vendor claim for Meta glasses is unattributed and model-unspecific: Sunny, Chinese media 2026-09-20.

## 5. Cadence observations

- **Capacity statements lead volume by years.** 1.5M/month was planned for mid-2022 and >1M/month for end-2022, against about 200k/month installed assembly in Oct 2025, while shipped volumes were far smaller. The only per-quarter unit disclosures found are from 2019 (6.5k → 15.6k → 109k TLens per quarter). Since then poLight reports only NOK revenue, end-market split and PO values.

## Return summary

No public customs or bill-of-lading source shows TLens or poLight shipments. ImportYeti covers only US sea imports: it has no poLight entity, its "Q Technology" is an unrelated US LED importer, and Meta's visible US manifests stop on 2025-06-09 with a "Manifest Confidentiality" marker. Comtrade and PSA totals are far too coarse, since 1M TLens would be only about US$2m.

The supply chain itself is confirmed. Wafers come from ST's 8-inch fab in Agrate, Italy. Assembly and test are at Tong Hsing Philippines in Calamba, Laguna: poLight's slides say "Theil line" and the CEO says "Tongxing". That line has about 200k/month assembly and 400–500k/month test, set to double by end-2026 with the Q Tech line.

Q Tech's Philippine plant is Actutek in Batangas (ex-TDK), which makes VCM/OIS actuators, not modules. The "likely coming from the Philippines" remark is from 2026-02-25, not 2026-08-19, and it means TLens from Tong Hsing.

The best ramp signal is The Elec: SeeYA has made Phoenix panels since May 2026, about 1M by late August. Watch poLight's Q3 report on 2026-10-29.
