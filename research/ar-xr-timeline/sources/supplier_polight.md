## 1. Profile

poLight ASA (Tønsberg, Norway; founded 2005; IPO on Oslo Børs 2018-10-01; ~27 FTE + 19 long-term consultants at end-2025) sells **TLens®**, a tunable lens in which a thin-film **PZT piezo MEMS** actuator bends a glass membrane over an optical polymer, changing optical power without moving parts (poLight claims <1 mW lens power, ~1 ms response, constant field of view, >1 billion focus cycles). Variants: TLens Silver / Silver Premium, Packaged TLens, **TLens Add-In** (placed *inside* the fixed lens stack, enabling wide-FOV reference designs such as 48 MP 1/1.5" 120° F/2.2) and Add-On modules, plus the industrial **MLens®** (M12, launched 2026-01-20) and the display-side **TWedge®** wobulator (pixel-shifting for AR light engines, still a pre-product in technical-sample stage TS5). Supply chain is fabless: gel/polymer made at HQ → PZT MEMS wafers from a single MEMS fab (inventory "for several millions TLens") → assembly/test at a partner line in the Philippines (~200k/month assembly, 400–500k/month test, ramp plan 1M/month) and, since 2025, a second line at **Q Technology (HK:1478)**, which bought 32.97% of poLight (NOK 171.5m at NOK 2.69) on 2025-04-15 "with backing from a top tier U.S. consumer electronics customer". poLight's relevance to AR/VR/XR glasses: TLens is already in enterprise AR/MR devices (Magic Leap 2 world-facing 13 MP camera per Yole teardown, Vuzix Shield, LLVision LEION Pro, a high-end MR HMD, SnkeXR), AR|MR is 70–86% of revenue since Q4 2025, and three separate consumer AR|MR programs are public: (A) a Q Tech-backed "top tier U.S. consumer electronics OEM" qualification program (POs 2025-08-06, 2026-02-11, 2026-06-18), (B) a "leading consumer OEM" case that became poLight's **first consumer AR|MR design-in** (POs 2025-11-24, 2026-04-07, 2026-05-20, 2026-07-10; product release expected 2026), and (C) a ~NOK 5m camera-design program for "several AR head-worn devices" for a top-tier U.S. OEM (2025-10-13; first camera modules shipped end-Q2 2026). CEO Øyvind Isaksen confirmed on 2026-08-19: "Those three are different, but there are more."

## 4. Camera / autofocus relevance and the qualification → design-in wording

**What TLens is in a glasses camera.** poLight's AR/MR page describes the use cases as "fast focus image capture of the environment the user actually sees regardless of the ambient light condition and the distance users look at" and "in-focus barcode and object recognition (for AI) at close distance or focus tuning of laser projection display" (https://www.polight.com/markets/augmented-reality/). Devices listed by poLight as using TLens: Magic Leap 2 Enterprise (world-facing camera), LLVision LEION Pro (world-facing), an unnamed "High-end MR HMD", Vuzix Shield (2× world-facing cameras). Reference designs with the Add-In (TLens inside the lens stack, which is what keeps the FOV constant and allows wide FOV): 48 MP 1/1.5" 120° F/2.2; 33 MP 1/3.6" 82° F/1.9; 32 MP 1/2.8" 87° F/2.3 (https://www.polight.com/products/tlens/). Website claims: "<1 mW", "response time is 1 ms", ">1 billion" focus changes (https://www.polight.com/technology/). The <6 mW-with-driver vs >180 mW VCM comparison in the user's brief is poLight's reference comparison for a 32 MP 1/2.8" module (not re-fetched here).

**Supply-chain position.** "From Gel > MEMS Wafer > TLens > Camera module > OEM": poLight sells bare/packaged TLens plus driver ASIC to camera-module makers (Q Tech is one; "poLight's strategy has always been to support all camera module partners"), but prices are negotiated with the OEM: "it is the OEM… who is kind of the big boss… negotiating all key components… and the camera module guy will be informed" (post-Q2 Q&A). Consumer price "around $2 +" per TLens; sample-stage pricing 2–4× that.

**poLight's own stated alternatives/competitors** (it never names Optotune, Corning Varioptic, CML or Sheba in these documents): fixed focus (most shipping glasses), **VCM** ("very tough competition from VCM… We are not able or willing to sell at the cheap VCM price level. We also do not think VCM is a good solution"), "other tunable optics… the liquid crystal guys… the liquid guys", and on 2026-09-10 "VCMs, liquid lenses, and metalenses" plus computational optics. Quarterly reports repeat: "various competing autofocus (AF) solutions will be implemented, depending on the specifications needed and cost sensitivity."

**Order size over time.** Program A tickets are flat-to-smaller (1.7 potential → 1.0 → 0.7): consistent with repeated qualification batches, not with a ramp. Program B jumped 0.9 → 2.4 then settled at 0.8/0.8 — consistent with the CEO's description of a deliberately large first mass-production PO followed by inventory-minimising call-offs; its unit volumes are not disclosed but at "$2+" per lens NOK 4.9m ≈ 200k+ lenses at most (upper bound; sample pricing would imply far fewer). Program C's NOK 5m is explicitly a design/NRE engagement, not units.

**Lead times observed.** Q Tech deal (2025-04-15) → first qualification PO 113 days; qualification PO → first deliveries ~Q4 2025 (~3–4 months); Program B "mature PoC" PO (2025-11-24) → design-in label 228 days; AR camera design PO → validation hardware 260 days; Q Tech line "all equipment installed" (Oct 2025) → "lenses tested and qualified" (Aug 2026) ≈ 10 months of process tuning. Capacity words move ahead of orders: doubling of assembly/test by end-2026 while disclosed consumer POs are < NOK 3m/quarter.

**What the sequence typically implies in optics supply chains** (interpretation, not a poLight statement): samples (priced 2–4×) → funded design/NRE (Program C) → component qualification batches with staged call-offs (Program A: 50% firm, 50% on continuation; then follow-ons as reliability/optical test gates are passed) → OEM/module-maker production-readiness audits (Q Tech line qualification; OEM buying its own optical test gear) → **pilot/mass-production-priced POs** (Program B: the price point, not the NOK size, is what poLight uses to call "mass production") → design-in → product announcement/launch (= poLight's "design-win") → monthly call-offs against forecasts. For a spring-2027 consumer launch the industry norm is tooling/PVT builds in Q4 2026–Q1 2027, so if Program A were that product one would expect its wording to move from "qualification" to production-priced POs by roughly end-2026/Q1 2027 — exactly the window poLight itself flags for "more visibility". As of 2026-10-06 that transition has **not** been announced; the only production-priced consumer AR\|MR orders are Program B's.
