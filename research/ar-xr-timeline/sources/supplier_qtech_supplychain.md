# Q Technology (1478.HK), the glasses camera-module supply chain, and the analyst layer

Research date: 2026-10-06. Scope: Q Tech's XR disclosures 2023→today; the DBS/Nordic analyst layer; module/assembly suppliers for Meta/Apple/Google-Samsung/Snap/Xreal glasses; ST as poLight's PZT-MEMS foundry; teardowns. Primary documents were downloaded and text-extracted (Q Tech decks in Chinese and English from qtechsmartvision.com, HKEX PDFs, poLight reports/prospectus, DBS public page). ## 1. Profile

Q Technology (Group) Co. Ltd (HKEX: 1478, "Q Tech"; Chinese 丘鈦科技) is a Kunshan-based maker of camera modules (CCM) and fingerprint modules for Android phones, automotive, IoT (drones, action cameras) and, since 2023-24, XR (pancake lenses, AR light engines, VST/6DoF/RGB camera modules). Its controlling shareholder is Chairman He Ningning (≈63% via BVI vehicle "Q Technology Investment Inc."). In 2025 Q Tech became poLight ASA's largest shareholder (32.97% at completion on 2025-06-04, diluted to 29.96% by end-2025 and ~29.90% by mid-2026) and committed to build a dedicated TLens assembly-and-test line; in parallel the controlling shareholder's vehicle bought TDK's camera-module-actuator business (Actutek, Taiwan/Philippines; framework 2025-04-28, completed by 2026-06-01). Q Tech's own decks place the XR customer story on a ladder: "Acquired vendor code of major US customer" (Mar 2024) → "Obtained US M brand Vendor code … target to crack in within this year" (Aug 2024) → "Qualified as the supplier for multiple international leading manufacturers and involved in the design of their VR/AR/AI glasses" (Mar 2025) → TLens-AF VST/SWIS cameras on the roadmap (Aug 2025) → "small-batch deliveries for 2 leading overseas terminal clients" (Mar 2026) → "TLens + AR Mono Engine: Volume Delivery Achieved … Overseas Key Customers: Ramp in 2H26 / 2027" (Aug 2026). It is therefore the single most important public "bridge" between poLight and a US glasses OEM, but no Q Tech filing names Meta.

## 4. Camera / autofocus relevance

- **What Q Tech actually says it ships for XR**: pancake lenses (MP since 2024), VR-Pancake light engine ("exclusively supplied"), dToF modules, "world's smallest VST module" for AI glasses (Mar 2025), Micro-LED display engine (small batch Mar 2026, pilot Aug 2026), AR monochrome light engine and **TLens — "Volume Delivery Achieved"** (Aug 2026). The Aug-2025 roadmap explicitly lists **"VR VST TLens AF camera"** and **"AR SWIS TLens AF camera"** next to VCM-AF and fixed-focus variants, i.e., Q Tech offers both TLens and VCM autofocus for VST (video-see-through) cameras — exactly the camera type a passthrough "VR glasses" product would use.
- **Actuator alternatives inside the Q Tech group**: via the controlling shareholder, Q Tech now sources VCM/OIS/variable-aperture actuators from Heyuan Yova, Xiamen ZH and the ex-TDK **Actutek** (Taiwan R&D, Philippines plant), so "Q Tech module" does not by itself imply TLens; the TLens-specific line and the Aug-2026 "TLens … volume delivery" wording are what tie poLight in.
- **Teardowns**: none of the published teardowns of Meta glasses (iFixit Oakley HSTN 2025-08-21; iFixit Meta Ray-Ban Display 2025-10-08) identify the camera-module maker or any AF actuator; all Meta glasses to date list a 12 MP (fixed-focus, ultra-wide) camera. poLight itself (Q3-2025 deck) says glasses/headsets "mainly use fixed-focus cameras". The only teardown-confirmed TLens win in XR remains **Magic Leap 2** (13 MP picture camera; Yole, 2023-03-02). iFixit's Vision Pro teardown (2024-02-03) lists 12 cameras but no module suppliers; rumored Vision Pro module suppliers are Cowell (sensor modules), Largan (lenses), LG Innotek/Sony (per general reporting), Luxshare (assembly). No teardowns were found for Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2, Xreal One/Eye (Xreal Eye is an optional 12 MP clip-on camera, $99), Snap Spectacles 2024 or Galaxy XR (iFixit device pages have no guides for them).
- **ST's role**: ST's Agrate (Italy) 8-inch thin-film-PZT MEMS line has fabricated the TLens actuator since 2014/15 (poLight is ST's first TFP customer; poLight owns the process IP and says it could second-source). ST's public marketing lists "camera autofocus" among TFP applications; ST and poLight were paired at an A*STAR PiezoMEMS event on 2026-09-25. No ST statement specifically about XR/tunable lenses was found.

## 5. Cadence observations

- **Q Tech's XR disclosure ladder (semi-annual decks, Mar/Aug)**: vendor code (2024-03) → "US M brand" vendor code + "target to crack in within this year" (2024-08, 5 months) → qualified supplier "involved in the design of their VR/AR/AI glasses" (2025-03, 7 months) → TLens-AF cameras on roadmap, "2026: TLens" (2025-08) → small-batch deliveries to 2 overseas clients (2026-03) → "TLens … Volume Delivery Achieved", "Ramp in 2H26/2027" (2026-08). That is ~24 months from vendor code to first small-batch deliveries and ~30 months to "volume delivery", consistent with a 2-to-2.5-year qualification→production arc.
- **Q Tech TLens line**: agreement 2025-04-15 → "launch expected in 2025" (prospectus, Jun-2025) → "all equipment installed … qualification of processes ongoing" (Nov-2025, ~7 months) → NOK 3.0m NRE booked in Q1-2026 → "lenses being produced, tested, and qualified", visibility end-2026 (Aug-2026, ~16 months). Line qualification is therefore running roughly 9–12 months behind the "launch 2025" wording.
- **Orders from the Q Tech-linked US OEM** (poLight): 2025-08-06 (≈NOK 1.7–1.8m, TLens+driver) → 2025-10-13 (NOK 5m, "designing a TLens-based camera for AR") → 2026-02-11 (final call ≈NOK 1m) → 2026-06-18 (NOK 0.7m, deliveries 2H26): intervals of ~2, 4 and 4 months; all explicitly "qualification", none yet "design-in" for this OEM, while a different consumer OEM converted to design-in in Aug-2026 after three orders in ~4 months (Apr 7 / May 20 / Jul 10, 2026).
- **Corporate M&A cadence (actuators)**: TDK framework 2025-04-28 → carve-out into Actutek 2026-01-01 → acquisition closed 2026-03-31 / share transfer 2026-06-01 → connected-transaction framework 2026-08-03 → EGM 2026-09-11: ~16 months from framework to full integration, i.e. the group's actuator platform is in place ahead of a 2H26/2027 ramp.
- **OEM announce→ship**: Meta Ray-Ban Display 2025-09-17 → 2025-09-30 (2 weeks); Meta VR Glasses 2026-09-23 → spring 2027 (~6–8 months, a Vision-Pro-style pre-announce). A spring-2027 launch implies module volume builds in roughly Q4-2026/Q1-2027, which lines up with Q Tech's "Ramp in 2H26/2027" and with DBS's (unverified) "1H27".
