# Chat digest: poLight TLens and Meta's spring-2027 VR Glasses

Merged from 9 independently extracted chunks of a ChatGPT (GPT-6 Pro) research thread dated 25-27 September 2026. Message indices in brackets refer to the thread.

Counts: 115 events, 59 entities, 44 probability estimates, 51 claims, 31 open questions.

## Summary

The thread tests one thesis: that poLight's TLens piezo-MEMS tunable lens is the autofocus element in the stereo passthrough cameras of Meta's ~100 g 'Meta VR Glasses', announced at Connect on 23 September 2026 for a spring-2027 launch at USD 1,299.99. Meta's developer specifications confirm 'color stereoscopic (26 PPD) passthrough with autofocus and depth sensing' but name no actuator or supplier, so the question is purely supplier identity.

Strongest evidence for poLight: Meta's granted patent US 12,663,619 (granted 23 June 2026) claims a flexible membrane, hard substrate, deformable polymer and thin-film PZT actuators - essentially TLens's architecture - and cites three 2022 poLight documents; a documented chain in which an anonymous top-tier U.S. OEM that had qualified TLens for years backed Q Technology's ~NOK 171m investment and dedicated TLens line (April 2025), followed by qualification POs (Aug 2025, Feb 2026, Jun 2026) for 'very advanced, high-end glasses' that could 'ramp in huge volumes'; Q Tech's 2024 'US M brand' XR vendor code; DBS Research (26 Aug 2026) stating a Meta project combining Q Tech modules with poLight's tunable lens enters mass production in 1H27; and a former Meta engineer's LinkedIn profile citing a 'miniature piezoelectric tunable lens AF camera module' that passed AR/VR/MR reliability qualification.

Strongest counter-evidence: poLight has never named Meta; its CEO confirmed on 19 Aug 2026 that the three headline programs are different end customers, so clues cannot be stacked; the 2026 design-in targets a 2026 product; the Q Tech program is still 'qualification' with only ~NOK 2.5m of attributable orders; DBS gives no source and disclaims verification; Meta's patents could describe an in-house or third-party PZT lens; CML, Optotune and VCM makers cannot be excluded.

Final probability: estimates oscillated 30-50% -> 62% -> 68% -> 78% -> 65% -> 88% -> 85% before settling at ~70% (60-65% without DBS) that TLens is in the spring-2027 Meta VR Glasses, with ~90% that Meta is the Q Tech-backed OEM. Not covered: teardowns or any direct Meta/poLight/Q Tech confirmation, full USPTO file wrappers, CML's latest accounts, Q Tech primary filings linking the 2024 M-brand customer to the 2025 investment, poLight's cumulative 2026 order total, actual Meta volumes or camera power figures, and the LinkedIn individual's identity.
