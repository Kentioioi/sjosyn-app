# Meta Reality Labs — the autofocus-camera program behind "Meta VR Glasses", reconstructed from public signals

Compiled 2026-10-06 (round 2). Scope: job postings, search-engine-indexed public profile snippets and public posts, Meta's own VR Glasses material (Connect 2026, developer docs, blog), supplier reports, earlier Meta devices, and the Lidu Huang tenure question. Everything established in round 1 (BRIEF2.md) is cited only where this file adds to it. Relative posting ages ("2 weeks ago") were observed on 2026-10-06 and are converted to "~" dates. No log-in sites were used; LinkedIn material is limited to what search engines or public job pages expose.

## 1. Profile

Meta Reality Labs' "Cameras and Depth" / "Camera Depth Sensor" organisation in Sunnyvale and Burlingame designs its own camera, depth and illuminator modules "end-to-end, from architecture and requirements through design, qualification, and mass-production ramp", and has them built by "offshore vendors and module integrators" (Meta job pages, Sept–Oct 2026). Inside it, a camera-actuator group led by an ex-Apple/ex-Western Digital "Camera Actuator Architect" (Lidu Huang, Meta late-2020 → Apr 2025) filed a cluster of autofocus patents whose priority dates run from Jan 2022 to Dec 2024: VCM power-saving wristband cameras (2022), a piezo-membrane tunable lens inside the lens barrel (Jul 2022–Aug 2023), a **gaze-driven autofocus passthrough camera** (priority 2023-03-31, granted 2026-02-10), a moving-single-element alternative (Dec 2023), in-field AF-actuator calibration (Sep 2024) and a tunable-lens-plus-wafer-level-optics camera module (Dec 2024). The first product to expose this work is **Meta VR Glasses** (announced 2026-09-23, spring 2027, US$1,299.99), whose developer spec reads "Color stereoscopic (26 PPD) passthrough with autofocus and depth sensing" and which has four-camera eye tracking — the sensing input the gaze-driven AF patent requires. No Meta document, job posting or press report names the actuator technology or the camera-module supplier; the piezo-tunable-lens filings cite poLight's TLens documents (round 1), and one Meta filing quotes "approximately 6 mW", "approximately 2 ms", "approximately 10 cm to infinity" and "no moving parts" for its variable lens.

## 4. Camera / autofocus relevance

**What Meta has said.** Only developer-facing pages carry the word "autofocus" ("Open-periphery color passthrough with autofocus and depth sensing"; "Color stereoscopic (26 PPD) passthrough with autofocus and depth sensing"). Consumer posts say "passthrough camera" (singular) and "full-color passthrough". No Meta statement gives camera count, resolution, FOV, focus mechanism or supplier; no SDK v207 API exposes focus distance; no Connect 2026 session covered the cameras. Reviewer-level facts: "four cameras that enable hand tracking and passthrough video" (Engadget), four eye-tracking cameras (UploadVR), near-field passthrough praised (Road to VR).

**What Meta's own IP says about how the autofocus could work.** Four distinct architectures are on file, all from the same Sunnyvale camera/actuator group:
1. *Piezo-membrane tunable lens inside the barrel* (round-1 cluster US 12,663,619 / 12,631,847 / 2026/0276939 / 2024/0151882 / 2025/0076634) — plus, new here, the abandoned omnibus US 2024/0297972 whose variable-lens section lists "MR pass-through RGB cameras" and quotes "approximately 2 ms", "approximately 6 mW", "approximately 10 cm to infinity", "no moving parts", and the 2026/0082130 calibration filing that names a "piezoelectric (PZT) actuated tunable lens" as the calibrated element. These are TLens-class figures (poLight advertises <6 mW incl. driver; the user's brief), but the text never names poLight.
2. *Gaze-driven focus control* (US 12,547,244, granted 2026-02-10): eye tracking → scene depth → left/right autofocus cameras; actuator-agnostic. Meta VR Glasses' four-camera eye tracking and "eyes and hands as primary input" make this the natural control layer for the product's autofocus passthrough.
3. *Moving single-element AF* (US 2025/0189869, priority 2023-12-06): a MEMS/spring-driven inner lens with ±2.5–4 D for passthrough — Meta's own non-tunable-lens fallback, citing LG Innotek/Samsung/Nidec art.
4. *Tunable lens + wafer-level optics* (US 2026/0177877, priority 2024-12-19; ex-Apple inventors): "polymer tunable lens technology or liquid lens technologies" — a newer, smaller module concept aimed at "head-mount devices", consistent with a glasses-class camera.
Plus MEMS Alvarez lenses for the *eye-tracking* cameras (US 12,625,351).

**What the hiring says.** Six camera-module roles went up within ~2 weeks of Connect 2026 (2026-09-22 → 10-05): Camera System Engineer (NTI→NPI, "qualification, and ramp", "offshore vendors and module integrators"), Opto-Mechanical Engineer, Camera ("Active Alignment (AA)", "ramp-up for mass production"), Camera and Depth Electrical Engineer ("auto-focus electrical design", "concept phase to mass production", "Module Integrator (MI) partners"), Lead Sensor HW & Integration (next-gen smart glasses), Technical Lead Camera IQ ("passthrough video applications"), Software Engineer Camera Systems ("3A"). None mentions tunable lens, piezo or a supplier. The only Meta posting naming an actuator type is the mid-2025 Seoul "Optical Systems Engineer" (preferred: "AF camera actuator (e.g. VCM)"), on a display/optics team.

## 5. Cadence observations

- **Patent priorities for AF on headsets run continuously from 2022-07 to 2024-12** (piezo cluster 2022-07→2023-08; gaze-control 2023-03-31; moving-element alternative 2023-12-06; actuator calibration 2024-09-17; tunable+WLO 2024-12-19). A spring-2027 product sits ~4.7 years after the first piezo priority and ~2.3 years after the last AF priority — inside Meta's usual 2–5-year filing-to-product lag. Grant lag on this family is ~2.0–3.4 years (gaze AF 2024-02→2026-02; barrel 2023-06→2026-05; wide-FOV 2023-02→2026-06).
- **Inventor continuity:** the "Camera Actuator Architect" is on every piezo/AF filing from 2022 to the 2024-09-17 calibration provisional, then exits in 2025-04; filings continue with the same co-inventors (Xiong, Te Hu, Brookmire, Likai Li) and, from Dec 2024, ex-Apple optics people (Magen, Kleiman Shwarsctein) — the programme outlived its architect, which is what a transition from NTI to NPI looks like.
- **Hiring burst:** 6 camera-module postings in the 14 days around Connect (T-6 to T-8 months before "spring 2027"), explicitly about qualification, ramp, AA and module-integrator factory support. Without archived 2024–2025 postings (gap) it cannot be shown whether this is a VR-Glasses ramp wave or routine churn for the next glasses generation ("next generation smart glasses", "AI/AR/VR devices" wording suggests both).
- **Leak-to-announce cadence for Phoenix:** firmware images Aug 2026 → renders 2026-09-12 → supply-chain story 2026-09-20 → announcement 2026-09-23 → developer SDK 2026-09-24: a 6-week run-up, with the camera supply chain still unreported.
- **Meta disclosure pattern:** autofocus is disclosed only in developer specs; Meta gave no hardware-deep-dive at Connect 2026 (contrast Connect 2022's Cambria passthrough explainer). Expect the actuator identity to surface from teardowns (≈2–8 weeks after retail) rather than from Meta.
