## 1. Snap Inc. / Specs Inc. (Spectacles → Specs)

### 1.1 Profile
Snap Inc. (NYSE: SNAP) has shipped camera glasses since 2016 (Spectacles gen 1–3, fixed-focus photo/video cameras) and AR-display glasses since 2021 (gen 4, developer-only) and 2024 (gen 5 "Spectacles '24", developer-only, 4 cameras: 2 RGB + 2 IR). On 2025-06-10 Snap committed to a consumer product, **Specs**, "in 2026"; it created a wholly-owned subsidiary **Specs Inc.** on 2026-01-28, signed a multi-year Qualcomm Snapdragon deal on 2026-04-10, unveiled Specs at AWE on 2026-06-16 (US$2,195, 51° FOV, 132/136 g, dual Snapdragon) and opened formal pre-orders on 2026-09-16 with shipping "later this fall" in the US, UK and France. Specs is a standalone see-through AR computer (no puck); Snap has not published camera megapixels, focus type, or any camera-module/actuator supplier. An initial production run of ~100,000 units has been reported by press.

### 1.4 Camera / autofocus relevance
- Spectacles '24 (gen 5): 2 full-colour RGB cameras (stereo capture) + 2 IR computer-vision cameras + depth sensor; resolution and focus type never published by Snap (Road to VR 2024-09-17; Guttag 2025-08-07).
- Specs (2026): four cameras reported (2 RGB + 2 IR); Snap's spec page and newsroom give **no megapixel or focus-type figure**; a recording LED is documented. No teardown yet (first units only reaching customers in Oct 2026).
- Supplier evidence: none for the camera module or any actuator. Known Snap optics suppliers are in-house (WaveOptics waveguides, acquired 2021) and Compound Photonics LCoS; chips Qualcomm Snapdragon. Gen-1 teardowns (2017) show Ambarella/Kingston/Nordic; FCC test firm Cerpass (Suzhou). **Contract manufacturer not identified in any source found.**
- Relevance to AF suppliers: Specs is a standalone consumer AR glasses with RGB photo cameras launching in the same window (fall 2026) as poLight's unnamed consumer AR/MR customer's product — a candidate only; no evidence links them.

### 1.5 Cadence observations
- Spectacles generations: Nov 2016 → Apr 2018 (17 mo) → Nov 2019 (19 mo) → May 2021 (18 mo) → Sep 2024 (40 mo) → Sep 2026 Specs pre-order (24 mo).

- Pattern: Snap runs an AWE (June) announce → September launch rhythm, with CES (January) demos in between.

## 2. Xreal (formerly Nreal)

### 2.1 Profile
Xreal (Beijing/Shanghai; HKEX listing applied 2026-04-01) is the volume leader in birdbath "display glasses" (Air, Air 2, One, One Pro, 1S) with 133,700 AR glasses sold in 2025 (111,400 One-series at ASP RMB 3,196), RMB 516m revenue and a cumulative RMB 2.05bn loss. Its optional **Xreal Eye** 12MP camera module (US$99, July 2025) is **fixed-focus** per Xreal's own spec page. Its flagship for 2026 is **Aura**, the first optical-see-through Android XR glasses with Google (announced 2025-05-20, revealed 2026-06-16, fall-2026 ship, ≤US$1,500), with 2 tracking cameras + 1 "high-resolution camera" whose resolution/focus type are undisclosed. Luxshare Precision is a shareholder.

## 4. RayNeo (TCL Electronics' AR brand)

### 4.1 Profile
