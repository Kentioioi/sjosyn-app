Amounts in NOK unless stated. Sources: Cision (news.cision.com/polight, mb.cision.com attachments), the Oslo Børs attachment mirror on storage.mfn.se, poLight's old Q4 Inc. poLight's own vocabulary (defined in the Q1 2022 report, 2022-05-12): **PoC** = "activity at the customer qualifying/testing the TLens technology/product"; **design-in** = "the customer has started a product development project having the intention to use TLens in future product"; **design-win** = "the customer have decided to release a product to the market using TLens". Note that for the first consumer wins (2020) poLight used "design-win" only at product launch, and that "design-in" cases are routinely removed (Xenocor 2024, two enterprise AR cases 2024, LBS 2025).

---

## 1. Profile

poLight ASA (Horten → Tønsberg, Norway; IPO 2018-10-01) sells the TLens piezo-MEMS tunable lens; wafers come from STMicroelectronics (Agrate, Italy), the polymer is made in-house, assembly/test was at Tong Hsing (Taiwan) and, from 2020–2023, moved to a line in the Philippines. Between 2019 and 2024 it took TLens from first paid samples to 28 "design-wins" (end-2024 count), but the history is dominated by enterprise/low-volume devices: two children's smartwatches (2020), Honeywell's EX30 scan engine (2020), a MAXHUB webcam (2021), Magic Leap 2 (2022), one Meizu flagship (2023), LLVision Leion Pro (2023), a "high-end MR HMD" (2023), Vuzix Shield (2024) and the Mini2P microscope ecosystem (2024). Its AR history is relevant for the map because it shows how long each step took when the customer was a real AR/MR OEM: the first AR design-in was disclosed in Feb 2021, the first AR design-win (Magic Leap 2) came 9–10 months later, the product shipped 10 months after that, and the "3 design-wins expected in 1H 2022" materialised over 1, 16 and 28 months — with one (XVisio) never materialising at all.

## 5. Cadence observations (what the record says a step "normally" takes at poLight)

1. **Design-win declared at or after the customer's launch, not before.** In every pre-2025 case the "design-win" press release coincided with the product being on the market (watches, EX30, MAXHUB, Leion Pro, Vuzix Shield, MR HMD, PhenoSys) or with the first mass-production PO for a product the customer had already scheduled (ML2, Meizu). So on the map, a poLight design-win is a lagging indicator of the OEM's launch (lag −1 to +1 month), whereas a "design-in" leads the launch by anything from 1 month (LLVision) to never (LBS, Xenocor, XVisio).

2. **Design-in → launch distribution (10 enterprise/consumer cases 2020–2025):** 1.2, 2.8, 3.3, 6.2, 7.3, 13.3, 19.4 months for the seven that launched; three never launched after 20, 30 and 41 months. Median of launched cases ≈6 months; but the two cases most like a consumer OEM program (ML2 world-facing camera; the consumer LBS display) sat at 19 months and ∞. **For a consumer AR/MR glasses program the defensible expected window is 10–20 months from design-in, with a material (≈30%) probability of cancellation.**

3. **First PO → launch:** 4.8 (watch), 5.9 (XUN), 7.3 (EX30), 10.0 (ML2), 0–2 (MR HMD, Meizu announcement) months. Volume consumer launches (watches) were the fastest because the first PO was already PVT/initial-MP sized (33k units). Program A (first PO 2025-08-06) and Program B (first PO 2025-11-24) have had ≈14 and ≈10.4 months since their first PO without a launch — longer than every historical consumer case and beyond (A) or at (B) the ML2 enterprise pace of 10 months.

4. **Guidance slippage is systematic.** "Design-win potential 1H 2022" (Nov 2021) → one win came early (Dec 2021), the second in Apr 2023, the third in Apr 2024; "mid-2023" for Vuzix/XVisio/LLVision → Apr 2023, never, Apr 2024; EX30 "late Q2/early Q3 2020" → Oct 2020 (+1 quarter); Xenocor "2022" → "mid-2023" → cancelled; LBS "some years" → cancelled; smartphone "Q1 2022" → cancelled → Jun 2023. **Rule of thumb from the record: enterprise AR dates slipped 6–12 months; consumer dates slipped indefinitely or were cancelled; only the barcode and research/healthcare cases held to within a quarter.** Of the 19 AR projects of Nov 2021, three became products (ML2 2022, Leion Pro 2023, Shield 2024), the consumer LBS one died in 2025, and the rest are untraceable in the counts.

5. **Order cadence after launch is thin for enterprise AR.** ML2: NOK 0.36m → 1.2m (8.5 months) → cumulative 2.1m at 15 months → 0.35m at 23 months. MR HMD: ≈NOK 0.4m initial, ≈0.4m repeat 22 months later. Barcode is the steady one: EX30 produced ≈NOK 4.7m in the first 13 months and still reorders in year 5. Any consumer glasses program would therefore look very different in the feed: poLight's CEO described it in 2026 as "PO per month or per second month" once a product ships.

6. **Capacity words run years ahead of volume.** Planned assembly capacity: 1M/month (2011 SVTC) → "around 1,500,000 [per month] by mid-2022" (Nov 2021) → ">1 million towards the end of 2022" (Feb 2022) → installed ≈200k/month assembly, 400–500k/month test, "ramp-up plan… 1 mill per month" and "produced >1 mill" lifetime (Oct 2025) → "doubling" by end-2026 with the Q Tech line. Realised FY revenue: NOK 22.5m (2023), 9.6m (2024), 20.5m (2025) — i.e. the installed 2025 capacity (≈2.4M lenses/yr) is already an order of magnitude above shipped volumes. Capacity announcements are therefore not a volume signal; only POs are.

7. **Wafer-order cycle (ST):** a PO in 2020 was delivered Q4 2020–Q2 2021; the PO placed in Q2 2021 ("to secure materials for 2022/part of 2023") was delivered through Oct 2023 — ≈28 months, with the promised completion date slipping from "end 2022" → "H1 2023" → "end 2023". Inventory (mainly wafers at cost) went NOK 16.8m (2021) → 45.6m (2022) → 70.1m (2023) → 62.4m (2024, after NOK 20.3m obsolescence provision), and no wafers have been ordered since 2023 ("inventory for several millions TLens"). **A new ST wafer order would be the first hard sign of expected volume beyond a few million units; its historical lead time is 1–2.5 years, so an order would have to appear roughly a year before any multi-million ramp.**

8. **Capital-raise cadence:** Sep 2018 (NOK 130m IPO) → Apr 2020 (≈50m) → Sep/Oct 2021 (125m + 12.8m) → Apr/May 2023 (135m) → Apr/May 2024 (≈140m gross) → Jun/Jul 2025 (171.5m + 51.4m, Q Tech). Interval 13–19 months, each raise 18–24 months of cash burn; the 2024 raise was priced at NOK 2.3085 (−30% TERP), the 2025 strategic placement at NOK 2.69. Raises have always been need-driven, never tied to a specific announced ramp; the 2024 prospectus wording ("secure pivotal design wins… liquidity through 2026") is the only forward tie.

9. **What the 2025–26 programs look like against this baseline.** Program B's path (mature-PoC PO → design-in in 7.5 months → launch guided "2026") is faster than any historical consumer case but matches the ML2 pattern (first MP PO ≈9 months before GA). Program A's three staged qualification POs over 10 months with no design-in have no historical analogue at poLight; the nearest are the LBS display (sample/design POs for 21 months, then stall) and the Honeywell design-in (6.5 months to confirmation). The honest calibration is therefore wide: a design-in for Program A would be "on time" anywhere between late 2026 and 2027, and the record says the risk of the case ending as a "removed design-in" is real.
