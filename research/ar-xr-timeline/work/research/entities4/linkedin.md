# Public LinkedIn and job-post trail: Meta / Reality Labs and TLens / tunable-lens autofocus (round 4)

Research date: 2026-10-08. Method: public search engines only (WebSearch extended mode, Firecrawl web search, Bing/DDG-style operator variants with and without `site:linkedin.com`). LinkedIn profiles were not opened. Only search-engine snippets were read. Three public `/posts/` and `/pulse/` URLs returned by search were each opened once without login. The page loaded, and LinkedIn showed sign-in overlays. No people-search scrapers or connection lists were used. Private individuals are described by role. People are named only when the brief already names them publicly or they are named inventors on Meta patents (Lidu Huang, Saeid Bagheri, and Bagheri's co-inventor Sean DelVecchio). LinkedIn activity IDs were converted to UTC post timestamps with the standard Snowflake shift (`id >> 22` = ms since epoch). The one downloaded file is `dl_linkedin/polight_na_techsales.pdf`. It was converted with `pdftotext`, and its text is in `polight_na_techsales.txt`.

## 1. Profile

This file audits the "LinkedIn trail" that the user's earlier forum and ChatGPT research cited as evidence that Meta Reality Labs built a TLens/piezo tunable-lens autofocus camera. It also checks for job posts from 2025–2026. The search engines confirm most of the trail, but only partly. Two items are fully verified as text: Lidu Huang's "miniature piezoelectric tunable lens AF camera module" bullet, and a Bay Area module-process lead's "NTI dToF/ Tlens camera module process developments with dual Active Alignments" bullet. Search snippets now give Huang's full public employment chain: Western Digital → Apple (Dec 2017–Sep 2020) → Meta (Sep 2020–Apr 2025) → Science (Jun 2025–Apr 2026) → Tesla (Apr 2026–present). The Hon Hai TLens bullet is confirmed, but its date is only bounded to after Dec 2023. Two items are not publicly indexed: the Avegant/ex-Meta "DRI for TLens camera process development" line, and Saeid Bagheri's "led the autofocus work" post. His profile, Reality Labs role and earlier posts are verified. None of the snippets names poLight, and none names a supplier. "TLens" is poLight's trademark, so the two TLens bullets amount to a supplier naming. New job posts show Meta hiring for "focus strategy (fixed-focus vs. AF)", "AF/OIS actuator systems" and sourcing of "actuators (AF/OIS)" for smart glasses/AR in late Sep–early Oct 2026. None of them mentions tunable, piezo or liquid lenses. No new Q Tech, Goertek or Luxshare post mentions TLens.

## 4. Camera / autofocus relevance

- **The technology named in the trail is piezo / TLens, not VCM.** Huang's Meta-era bullets name a "miniature piezoelectric tunable lens AF camera module" that passed "full reliability qualifications for AR/VR/MR" and "high NTI yields ... readiness for production ramp". The same role also lists VCM AF, VCM OIS+AF and SMA OIS+VCM AF development. Meta's actuator team therefore evaluated all four classes (VCM, SMA, tunable, piezo). The module-process lead's bullet names "Tlens" explicitly, with dToF and "dual Active Alignments". Dual AA fits a TLens Add-In stack, where both the lens stack and the TLens element need alignment. That reading is an inference.
- **"NTI" is Meta-internal vocabulary.** Meta's Camera System Engineer post (dataset, 2026-09-22) says "new-technology initiative (NTI) through to new-product introduction (NPI)". Huang's "high NTI yields" and the module lead's "NTI dToF/ Tlens camera module" both use the same term. That makes it likely the module lead's TLens bullet comes from a Meta role, even though the snippet does not show the employer (inference, circumstantial). In Meta's usage, NTI is the pre-product technology stage, so neither bullet proves selection for a shipping product.
- **"u-OLED" in the same bullet list.** Meta VR Glasses use micro-OLED panels (dataset). The module lead's adjacent bullet on "u-OLED display bright dot pixel repair process development for next Gen" places that role on a VR/AR display-engine program. This also comes from a snippet with no date.
- **Bagheri.** Public facts: he is a Reality Labs camera software engineer (Google Scholar "Software Engineer, Meta Reality Labs"). He was a named inventor on a Meta 2025 auto-exposure (3A) patent, and in 2022 he posted that his team was "building and shipping some of the most ambitious camera systems ever". A Bing-indexed ZoomInfo page lists him as "Software Engineer (Camera)" at Meta, with prior AF tuning work at Motorola Solutions. That page was seen only through a search summary and not opened. It is consistent with his owning the AF software, but software ownership says nothing about the actuator type.
- **Meta job posts, Sep–Oct 2026.** Meta now has open roles that own "focus strategy (fixed-focus vs. AF)" and "AF/OIS actuator systems" for AR/VR cameras, plus a smart-glasses/AR sourcing role covering "actuators (AF/OIS)" and onboarding "new module integrators". The wording describes AF as a generation-by-generation architecture choice and stays neutral on technology: no tunable, piezo, liquid or MEMS terms. The Camera and Depth Electrical Engineer post (dataset) remains the only current post that says "auto-focus electrical design".
- **Supplier names.** No snippet, post or job ad names poLight, Q Tech, Goertek, Sunny or Luxshare together with Meta. "TLens" (a poLight trademark) appears in two profiles tied to Meta (module lead: Meta among employers; Huang's bullet says "piezoelectric tunable lens" without the trademark) and in one Hon Hai profile whose earlier role lists Meta as a customer.
