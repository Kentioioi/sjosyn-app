## 1. Profile

poLight's TLens is a piezo-thin-film (PZT) MEMS actuator bending a glass membrane over a polymer lens body on a glass support; it is sold as a packaged part and as an "Add-In" element placed inside a fixed lens stack. In the 2024–2026 patent record the literal string "TLens"/"T-Lens" is used as a generic noun by Chinese camera-module, lens and XR-device makers (Goertek's XR subsidiary, Q Tech, Sunny, Huaqin, Honor, Mojie, Kinko) and by Samsung, Bosch and ByteDance/Lemon, almost always as "可调透镜TLens" (tunable lens TLens) with the poLight construction (piezo film / glass membrane / polymer / glass support) spelled out. The highest-value hits for the project are: four **Goertek XR (青岛歌尔视界)** applications (filed 2024-06-03 and 2024-09-02) that *claim* a head-mounted-device camera "including a tunable lens TLens" for gaze-driven and focus-stacked passthrough/VST and display-less camera glasses; two 2026 **Q Tech** filings (a TLens diopter inline-QC method whose claim 10 recites the TLens stack, and a TLens+metalens AF/TOF module); a 2026 **Sunny Optical** AR/VR wearable front-camera lens with a "Tlens module" as its autofocus group; **Samsung**'s smart-glasses depth camera whose "focus-variable lens may be a tunable lens (T-lens)"; and **Huaqin**'s granted VR see-through camera lens with a "T lens" at the stop (F/2.1, 120° FOV, TTL 10 mm). Meta never writes "TLens" in its own text, but two previously unlisted Meta camera-module filings (priority 2024-12-19 and 2025-02-12, published 2026-06-25 and 2026-08-13) keep a surface-deformable tunable lens in a glasses camera and state that "existing camera modules use a large tunable lens … 3×3 mm or 5×5 mm" that is "glued on".

## 4. Camera / autofocus relevance — what the hits say about products

- **Goertek XR (Meta's principal Quest/Ray-Ban assembler per ChinaTalk and Road to VR reporting; not stated in the patents).** The two 2024-06-03 filings describe an HMD with an RGB/"variable-focus high-frame-rate" camera and an ET camera; the user "turns on the VST function", the ET camera supplies the fixation point and "the motor/TLens control circuit" sets focus; VCM focus of "close to 1 s" is called unacceptable for VST, and the TLens is described exactly as poLight's part (polymer deformed by a piezo film under voltage, driven via silver paste or FPC). The 2024-09-02 pair targets display-less camera glasses (no preview, fixed-focus today) and add an eye camera to set the T-Lens voltage from binocular gaze distance. Product classes covered: passthrough (VST) camera, display-less wearable camera; eye tracking is the input, not the TLens use.
- **Huaqin (ODM).** VR see-through camera lens with a T-lens at the stop: F/2.1, FOV ≥115° (120° preferred), TTL 10 mm, matched to an 8 M sensor, 20 cm–5 m, granted 2025-04-08. This is the only *granted* XR-camera patent in the set that names a T lens.
- **Samsung.** Smart-glasses second camera with a T-lens for depth-from-focus; the T-lens is drawn with piezo film, glass membrane, glass panel and polymer (poLight construction), priority 2022-10-31.
- **ByteDance/Lemon.** Gaze-driven focus in XR; "poLight lens" named both as an AF technology and for its passive thermal-compensation property.
- **Sunny Optical.** A 2026 AR/VR wearable front-camera lens explicitly designed around a "Tlens module" seated in its own barrel — a lens vendor productising TLens-front designs for wearables.
- **Q Tech.** 2026 filings cover inline diopter QC for TLens-structured parts "throughout mass production" and a TLens+metalens AF/TOF receiver — consistent with the Q Tech "VR VST TLens AF camera" product map and the 2026-08 "TLens + AR Mono Engine: Volume Delivery Achieved" deck noted in BRIEF2.
- **Meta.** No TLens string anywhere in Meta's text. The two new filings keep a surface-deformable, piezo-membrane-capable tunable lens inside a glasses camera, now paired with wafer-level optics and no barrel (2024-12 priority) and with an exposed front element plus shock-isolating sleeve (2025-02 priority, Lidu Huang inventor). The 2024-12 text's statement that existing modules use "a large tunable lens … 3×3 mm or 5×5 mm … glued on" to a "large form factor (e.g. 1 inch image sensor)" module is the closest thing to a description of an existing tunable-lens camera build. No Meta codename appears next to a TLens mention in any document found.
- **Bosch.** TLens named for the display path of retinal-scanning data glasses, not a camera.
