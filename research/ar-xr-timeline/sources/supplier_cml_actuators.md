# Mechanical-actuator competitors to poLight TLens: Cambridge Mechatronics (CML), Alps Alpine, MinebeaMitsumi, TDK→Q Tech (Actutek), and Korean/Chinese VCM makers

Compiled 2026-10-06. Scope: the mechanical (SMA / VCM / piezo-motor) autofocus-actuator suppliers that could compete with poLight's TLens for autofocus cameras in AR/VR/XR glasses. Dates are only stated where seen in a source. WebSearch budget was exhausted mid-task; the later part of the research relied on direct fetches of company pages, Google Patents, Companies House, SPIE proceedings pages and Firecrawl search (see Gaps).

---

## 1. Profile

**Cambridge Mechatronics Ltd (CML)**, Cambridge UK (Companies House 03071231, incorporated 1995-06-21 as "1... Limited", renamed 2008) designs shape-memory-alloy (SMA) wire actuators plus the controller ICs (CM824 etc.) and control firmware that drive them, and licenses the actuator designs to manufacturing partners (Alps Alpine, GalaxyCore, Liaoning Zhonglan/ZET, earlier TDK and Hutchinson/Foxconn) while selling its own silicon since 2023–24. Its SMA autofocus (AF), optical image stabilisation (OIS), AF+OIS "8-wire" and variable-aperture actuators ship in Huawei, Honor, Xiaomi, Meizu and Tecno phones; CML claims "100+ million consumer devices" (Oct 2025). For XR it markets "low power focus adjustment and enhanced display performance" — i.e. SMA actuators for varifocal / vergence-accommodation (VAC) displays, thermal focus compensation, pixel-shift "wobulation", and camera AF — with a "Zero Hold Power" (ZHP) friction-latch feature that lets an SMA actuator hold position with no current. CML has said since Oct 2021 that it has "multiple commercial engagements with the US technology giants" for "smartphones and AR/VR devices", and in Jan 2023 that it was "already engaged in commercial projects with several industry leaders" on XR focus compensation. It is the most credible mechanical-actuator alternative to TLens for an autofocus camera in glasses, but every XR statement is customer-anonymous; its publicly documented XR work is display-side (varifocal) as much as camera-side.

**Alps Alpine** (Tokyo) is a high-volume VCM camera-actuator maker that began mass production of CML-licensed SMA actuators in Jan 2023 and sampled a piezo zoom actuator the same month. **MinebeaMitsumi** (Mitsumi Electric) has made miniature AF VCMs since 2005 at Cebu, Philippines. **TDK** sold its "Micro Actuator Solution" (camera-module actuator) business — carved out of TDK Taiwan as "Actutek" — to Q Technology's controlling shareholder in a deal signed 2025-04-28 and scheduled to close by end-March 2026; this matters because Q Tech is simultaneously poLight's largest shareholder and TLens production partner, and the TDK unit includes "memory metal" (SMA) actuator IP. No public evidence was found of Samsung Electro-Mechanics, LG Innotek, Jahwa, Hysonic or Sunny Optical supplying autofocus *actuators* for XR glasses; Sunny Optical does claim the No.1 share in smart-glasses imaging modules (Aug 2025).

## 4. Camera / autofocus relevance

- **CML SMA for cameras:** 4-wire lens-shift OIS, sensor-shift OIS, slim AF, 8-wire lens-shift AF+OIS (6-DoF, mass production since Mate 50 Pro, Mar 2023), module-tilt OIS, variable aperture. Closed-loop position sensing by SMA wire resistance (no Hall sensors, no magnets → no EMI). For XR the Applications page lists "autofocus in cameras" alongside varifocal displays and athermalisation (https://www.cambridgemechatronics.com/en/sma-platform/applications/).
- **Power:** SMA needs current to heat the wire while moving/holding; CML's ZHP removes hold power via a friction latch (May 2025 blog). Only public numbers: ~3.2 V × ~20 mA (~64 mW) during continuous pixel shifting at CES 2023 (Guttag); ZHP example "<0.1 mA" average in a 5-transition/3-minute scenario — a variable-aperture use-case, not continuous video-passthrough AF. No public response-time (ms) figure.
- **Deformable-lens route:** WO2026087900A1 shows CML pursuing SMA-driven membrane/liquid-lens focus for "glasses and ... VR/AR headsets", i.e. a tunable-lens architecture, not only lens displacement. It remains an application; no product.
- **Alps Alpine:** CML-licensed SMA actuators in mass production since Jan 2023 (smartphones); piezo zoom actuator sampling Jan 2023; VCM lines since 2016. No XR camera announcement.
- **MinebeaMitsumi:** AF VCM / OIS for smartphones since 2005; no XR announcement.
- **TDK→Q Tech / Actutek:** TDK's camera-module actuator business (suspension-wire, guide-rod, SMA "memory metal", ball-type; 2,000+ patents) moved to Q Tech's controlling shareholder (Actutek Taiwan R&D, Actutek Philippines manufacturing). Implication: Q Tech now controls both a conventional/SMA mechanical-AF actuator house and (via its ~largest-shareholder stake, June 2025) poLight's TLens — so a Q Tech-built XR camera module could use either technology; Q Tech's own XR slide counts 1–3 camera modules per AR device and 1 per AI-glasses device.
- **Sunny Optical:** claims global No.1 share in smart-glasses imaging modules (Aug 2025) and showed own AI glasses at CES 2026 — a module-level competitor/customer, with no stated actuator technology.
- **Supplier clues for Meta glasses:** Meta patents US20230280637A1 (two-stage VCM + MEMS/SMA; abandoned May 2025) and US12493197B2 (granted Dec 2025) cite CML prior art (examiner-cited), and the latter also cites poLight. These are prior-art citations, not supply evidence.
