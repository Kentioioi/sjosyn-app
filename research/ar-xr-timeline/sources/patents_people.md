## 1. Profile

This file covers the intellectual-property and people trail rather than one company. The core of it is a cluster of **Meta Platforms Technologies** patents (priority dates Jan 2022 – Aug 2023, grants Dec 2025 – Jun 2026, a continuation published 17 Sep 2026) describing a piezo-membrane tunable-lens autofocus camera for headsets whose construction (rigid window / deformable polymer / deformable glass membrane / thin-film PZT actuator) matches poLight's TLens, and whose flagship grant (US 12,663,619) lists three poLight TLens documents retrieved by Meta's side on 19 Oct 2022. Around it sit poLight's own patent families (2006 → 2025), Cambridge Mechatronics' SMA filings for deformable lenses in AR/VR headsets (2024 → 2026), Optotune's liquid-lens filings for smart glasses, and Apple's tunable-lens filings (which are for see-through prescription lenses, not cameras). The people trail is anchored by the former Meta "Camera Actuator Architect" Lidu Huang (Meta Sep 2020 – Apr 2025; inventor on every Meta tunable-lens-in-barrel filing; LinkedIn snippet: "Led development of miniature piezoelectric tunable lens AF camera module … passing full reliability qualifications for AR/VR/MR"), plus ex-CML CTOs now at Apple and a Q Tech executive on poLight's board.

## 4. Camera / autofocus relevance

**What the Meta patents actually claim/describe.**
- *Architecture* (US 12,663,619; US 12,631,847; US 2026/0276939; US 2024/0151882; US 2025/0076634): a tunable element made of a rigid window, a deformable polymer, a deformable **glass** membrane and a thin-film piezo (PZT named) actuator, placed **inside** the fixed-lens stack/barrel, with electrical connection through the barrel and a "relatively high voltage" controller on the sensor PCB; closed-loop capacitance sensing of the piezo. Applications: headset imaging devices for depth sensing, passthrough RGB, AR/VR/MR; AR-glasses and VR-headset figures.
- *Optical spec* (US 12,663,619 claim 1): >100° diagonal FOV, F>2 (example F/2.2), EFL>2 mm, **TTL 5 mm**, BFL<1 mm, aperture stop on the tunable lens, IR-blocking coating on its hard substrate (claim 4), ~20 cm–∞ focus with 0/20/40 V.
- *Alternative tracks still alive at Meta*: (a) moving-lens actuators for a wristband camera (2022 filings; one granted Dec 2025, one allowed-then-abandoned May 2025); (b) a Nov-2025 filing for an LC/electrowetting tunable lens + wafer-level optics camera for AR glasses (secondary source). Meta is therefore not single-sourced on tunable-lens technology at the IP level.

**Why this reads as TLens-adjacent.** poLight's public TLens construction (glass membrane + optical polymer + rigid window + PZT thin film; <6 mW; Add-In inside the lens stack for low-TTL wide-FOV cameras) maps element-for-element onto Meta's claimed construction, and Meta's own IDS lists the TLens Add-In 120° reference design and Packaged TLens pages (retrieved 2022-10-19) plus poLight's MSTC-2022 PZT MEMS talk. Two older poLight patents (US10,473,900 Phair; US2020/0204740 Tallaron) are also in the reference list, and two Meta wristband-camera filings cite poLight's stress-distribution patent. What the patents do **not** show: any poLight part number, any statement that the element is purchased, or poLight's packaging (Meta claims its own via-through-barrel and FPCB/wire-bond integration).

**Supplier clues from the people trail.** The LinkedIn snippet of the inventor common to all barrel/tunable-lens filings (Lidu Huang; Meta Sep 2020 – Apr 2025; "Camera Actuator Architect" on ResearchGate) says the piezo tunable-lens AF module "passed full reliability qualifications for AR/VR/MR" and reached "readiness for production ramp" before April 2025 — i.e., the module was qualified, not just patented. The snippet does not name poLight, PZT, or a part number.

**Competitors' IP for glasses cameras.** CML's 2024–2026 filings move SMA toward deforming liquid/gel lenses with zero hold power explicitly for AR/VR headsets (WO2025186538/9, WO2026087900), but none claims a piezo membrane. Optotune's filings target liquid lenses for smart-glasses optics and gravity-coma compensation (WO2026002959). Apple's tunable-lens filings (Topliss/Burbridge/Pedder, 2023–2024) are for see-through prescription lenses in HMDs; no Apple glasses-camera AF tunable-lens filing surfaced. Samsung's tunable-lens filings found concern depth acquisition; Google and Snap: none found for camera AF hardware.

## 5. Cadence observations

- **Meta piezo tunable-lens priority dates cluster in 13 months**: 2022-07-22, 2022-07-26, 2022-11-08, 2023-02-08, 2023-03-06, (CIP 2023-07-26), 2023-08-28 — then nothing new on piezo until the Apr-2026 continuation. Reading: concept → optical design → integration → control electronics in ~1 year, with the design frozen by late 2023; this is consistent with reliability qualification during 2024 and "readiness for production ramp" by Apr 2025 (LinkedIn), and with a 2027 product under the usual 2–4-year priority-to-product lag (2022-07 → 2027-spring = ~4.7 years; 2023-02 → 2027-spring = ~4.2 years, at the long end, which fits a program that slipped from the cancelled wristband/early-glasses roadmap).
- **Before the piezo cluster, the same inventors filed moving-lens (VCM/SMA) camera AF for a wristband** (2022-01-04, 2022-05-31). The SMA-flavoured two-stage actuator application was allowed on 2025-01-21 and abandoned on 2025-05-08 for non-payment of the issue fee, i.e., Meta chose not to pay ~USD 1,200 to keep it, in the same month the Q Tech–poLight deal (2025-04-15) and the inventor's departure (Apr 2025) occurred. Circumstantial, but directionally against a moving-lens AF choice.
- **Grant pipeline**: filing→grant took 3.4 y (12,663,619; needed a final rejection + continued prosecution and a narrowing to the 5-mm-TTL spec), 2.9 y (12,631,847) and 3.4 y (12,493,197). Meta filed the barrel-via continuation 19 days before the parent issued (standard practice to keep the family open), and it published 6 days before the 2026-09-23 product announcement — coincidence of USPTO 18-month/continuation publication timing, not a signal.
- **poLight filing cadence**: ~68 publications with priority ≥2016; recent priorities 2023-06-29/30 (astigmatism stack; passive thermal compensation), 2024-04-26 (wobulation), 2024-07-16 (lead-free TLens; tilt assembly), 2024-09-02 (flare mitigation), 2024-10-29 (ring-actuated assembly), 2025-02-03 (low-power driver) — roughly one new family per quarter since mid-2024, with "lead-free" and "low-power driver" pointing at consumer-qualification requirements (RoHS, power budget).
- **CML XR IP**: SPIE talks on SMA for AR/VR every January 2022–2023, then XR-specific deformable-lens priorities 2024-03-04 and 2024-10-24 (published 2025-09 and 2026-04-30). Lag from CML's "commercial engagements with US technology giants" (Oct 2021) to its first deformable-lens-for-headset priority: ~2.4 years.
- **Conference cadence**: poLight CTO invited talk at SPIE AR/VR/MR two years running (Jan 2025, Jan 2026), both framed around AR/MR cameras; Meta plenary (Jason Hartlove) both years; CML last presented an XR talk there in Jan 2023; Optotune absent from the invited volumes 2025–2026.
