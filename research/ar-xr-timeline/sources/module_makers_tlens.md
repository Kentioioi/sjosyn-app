# Camera-module makers: public trail with TLens and XR autofocus cameras
Scope: Hon Hai/Foxconn (XR/HMD business, Varjo, Taoyuan camera-module teams), Sunny Optical, Goertek, Luxshare (+LuxVisions/Lite-On), LG Innotek, Cowell, Primax, Chicony, Q Tech module side; plus O-Film and Chongqing TS-Precision (TSP) where they surfaced as TLens module makers. Research date 2026-10-06. Round-1 facts (poLight orders, Q Tech decks, Meta patents, Meta VR Glasses specs) are not repeated except as cross-links.

## 1. Profile
The camera-module makers (CCMs) sit between poLight (TLens actuator) and the glasses/headset OEMs: the OEM dictates the camera spec, the CCM integrates lens + sensor + actuator and runs active alignment, and poLight (with Q Tech as assembly/wafer partner) supplies the TLens. The public trail shows four tiers of engagement. (a) Explicit TLens module work: Luxshare showed a "T-LENS Camera" (6P+1G lens, electrically controlled focusing) in its AR-glasses optics line-up at CIOE 2026 (9–11 Sept 2026); a Hon Hai Taoyuan AR/VR camera-module RD head lists "completed TLens fast-focus camera module driver development" alongside Varjo HMD mass production; O-Film has a "T-Lens miniaturised AF module" product page; Chongqing TS-Precision (TSP) markets an off-the-shelf TLens camera module; Sunny filed a 2021 patent integrating a "liquid lens (T-lens)" inside the AF/OIS motor. (b) Volume smart-glasses camera suppliers with no actuator disclosed: Sunny (global No.1 in smart-glasses imaging modules, 12MP RGB modules, AR-glasses camera-module revenue +800% in FY2025), Goertek (Meta/Xiaomi ODM, optics via Goertek Optics), Cowell (Apple Vision Pro cameras). (c) Capability builders: LG Innotek (Google AR camera-module programme 2022, Vision Pro ToF, AOE lens JV for XR), Primax (HOC miniature module aimed at AR/VR glasses), Q Tech (hiring an AR/VR whole-device manufacturing-centre head in Sept 2026 naming Meta/Pico as client examples). (d) No public XR-camera trail: Chicony, Lite-On (other than LuxVisions, now Luxshare-controlled). None of the CCMs has publicly confirmed TLens in a shipping consumer product; the Foxconn–Varjo, Foxconn–Microsoft/Google and Foxconn–Amazon camera-module relationships are documented only at role level and do not show TLens in any shipped unit.

## 4. Camera / autofocus relevance
- Luxshare (and LuxVisions): the only CCM that has publicly put a TLens-branded autofocus camera module on a trade-show stand (CIOE 2026, 9–11 Sept 2026). Company wording: "T-LENS Camera"; press wording: 6P+1G lens with "电控调焦" (electrically controlled focusing), fast response, stable FOV — consistent with TLens's constant-FOV claim. Attendee reports attribute it to poLight and to the world-facing camera of AI glasses (QR scanning use case). Luxshare's own Lark glasses specs cite Sony IMX681 (2025) and "第一视角高清摄像头" (2026); no statement that the T-LENS camera is inside Lark. Actuator type: piezo-MEMS tunable lens by name; no supplier named by Luxshare.
- Hon Hai/Foxconn: Taoyuan AR&VR camera/projector module RD head profile shows (i) Varjo HMD mass production (Varjo XR-4: dual 20 MP passthrough, announced 2023-11-27), (ii) ADI ToF module test platform, (iii) "TLens fast-focus camera module driver development", (iv) Google & Microsoft camera-module project leadership, Amazon/Google customer experience. Foxconn is a Varjo strategic investor (2022-09-06) and Varjo's CEO called it a potential manufacturing partner. No public evidence that Varjo XR-4, any Microsoft or Amazon device shipped with TLens; Varjo lists no AF for the 20 MP cameras.
- Sunny: No.1 in smart-glasses imaging modules (H1 2025), 12 MP RGB modules, AR-glasses camera-module revenue +800% (FY2025), says AR glasses require "compact high-resolution autofocus cameras" (H1 2026). Holds a 2021 patent with a T-lens inside the AF/OIS motor. Analysts (CMBI 2025-02, Citi 2026-09) name Sunny as camera supplier for Meta AI glasses and a VR Glasses beneficiary. No actuator supplier disclosed; round-1 Yole/Alps data show Sunny also builds its own VCM/SMA actuators ("integrated motor-module revenue" guided +50% for 2026).
- Goertek: Meta/Xiaomi glasses ODM and Quest assembler; optics via Goertek Optics (waveguides, Pancake, projection engines). Its "electrically tunable liquid-crystal lens" (CES 2026) is a display-side diopter/VAC lens, not a camera actuator. No public camera-AF actuator trail.
- Q Tech (module side): "VR VST TLens AF camera" on its product map (round 1); Sept 2026 hiring for a Kunshan XR whole-device factory naming Meta and Pico as client examples and listing camera modules among XR core materials; VCM-design and imaging-solution roles open.
- O-Film: catalogue "T-Lens 小型化AF模组" (since ~2020), smartphone/front-camera/watch framing, not XR-specific.
- TSP (Chongqing TS-Precision): off-the-shelf TLens camera module; poLight says a TSP consumer camera is on sale and TSP sampled a Chinese endoscope project (2025-10-30).
- LG Innotek: XR-camera capability (Google AR glasses programme 2022; Vision Pro ToF exclusive; AOE lens stake for XR modules 2024-01-18); no AF/actuator statement for glasses.
- Cowell: Vision Pro camera modules (2024), Apple AI-glasses hopes (2027); no AF detail.
- Primax: HOC miniature module marketed for AR/VR glasses (2024-08-29); no actuator detail. Chicony, Lite-On: no XR-camera trail found.

## 5. Cadence observations
- Sunny updates its glasses-module position every ~5 months (results 2025-03-24, 2025-08-19, 2026-03-30, 2026-08-27); tone moved from "first-mover advantage in shipment" (Mar 2025) to "No.1 share" (Aug 2025) to "+800% revenue" (FY2025) to "large-scale AR orders from top clients… from next year" (Aug 2026): a 12–18-month lag between first volume statements and a named 2027 ramp.
- Luxshare's CIOE cadence is annual (Sept): fixed-focus IMX681 glasses in Sept 2025 → TLens-branded AF camera in the component line-up in Sept 2026, i.e. one show-cycle from fixed focus to electrically tunable AF.
- Foxconn's TLens trail (driver development in a 2024 role) has produced no public product in ~24 months; the Varjo relationship ran investor (Sept 2022) → mass production (XR-4, Dec 2023) in ~15 months.
- LG Innotek's Google AR camera-module programme (Apr 2022) still has no shipping product after 4.5 years; the AOE lens partnership (Jan 2024) and AR module demo (Jul 2024) show ~6-month capability steps.
- Q Tech's XR factory hiring (28 Sept 2026) lands 1–5 months after its own "ramp in 2H26/2027" deck (24 Aug 2026) and five days after Meta VR Glasses (23 Sept 2026), consistent with staffing for a 2027 NPI→MP cycle.
- Analyst supplier attributions for Meta glasses cameras (CMBI Feb 2025, Citi Sept 2026) recur at ~19-month spacing and consistently name Sunny, never an actuator vendor.
