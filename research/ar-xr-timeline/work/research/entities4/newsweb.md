## 1. Profile
poLight ASA (Tønsberg, ISIN NO0012535832, LEI 2138007ZPDNUIHX6Z659) makes the TLens piezo-MEMS tunable autofocus lens and the TWedge wobulator, and in 2025–26 has pitched both mainly at AR|MR glasses. The share register changed shape in 2025. Q Technology (Group) Co. Ltd (1478.HK) took 63,743,112 new shares at NOK 2.69 (NOK 171.5m) and became the anchor holder, at 32.97% → 30.0% → 29.66%. A NOK 51.4m subsequent offering at the same price followed, and state VC Investinor exited in Nov 2025. Since then the register has been a stable Q Tech block of about 30%, a family holding company (LHH AS, about 5.8–6%), and a long retail tail. There were 7,401 VPS holders at end-2025, and Nordnet's nominee and insurance accounts hold about 10%. Insider activity is almost all option grants and exercises. The only open-market insider event in the period is a coordinated exercise-and-sell by the CEO, COO and CTO in Aug 2026. No net short position in PLT appears in Finanstilsynet's public short-sale register.

## 9. Interpretation: what insider and holder behaviour does and does not indicate
- **The Aug 2026 sale** was an exercise-and-sell of in-the-money options, in equal blocks of 369,862, through one broker. Each executive's pre-existing shares were untouched, and they still hold most of their options (CEO about 3.96m). In Norway, employee option gains are taxed as salary at exercise, so selling exercised shares is a common way to fund that tax. **It is weak evidence of a negative view**, and it should not be read as positive either. Per AR2025, the board opens exercise windows after reports. Under MAR, insiders may not trade while holding inside information, so the window suggests the company judged it had no undisclosed price-sensitive information during 14–26 Aug. It says nothing about information the company did not have, such as Meta's own Connect plans. The CFO did not participate.
- **No insider buying.** Executives get exposure through options, and the directors, including the Q Tech nominees, hold no shares. The lack of open-market buys around the design-in or Connect is therefore uninformative.
- **Q Tech** has neither bought nor sold since the placement, but it is contractually locked up to about 2027-06-04, so its inaction carries no signal. Moving an Executive Director for finance, securities and risk onto the board (2026-08-13) is consistent with closer oversight, but was explained as an internal reshuffle.
- **Institutions**: no new 5% holder appeared, and no fund crossed a flagging threshold in 2026. Nordea trimmed. DNB's disruptive fund has held 2.0m flat. Handelsbanken's index fund rose mechanically. The register is retail-dominated (7,105–7,401 holders; Nordnet accounts about 10%), so price moves around Connect were not driven by disclosed institutional flows.
- **Short register**: no disclosed short of 0.5% or more, so there is no visible bearish hedge-fund positioning, but small or unreported positions cannot be ruled out.
- **Bottom line**: none of these market signals confirm or refute TLens in Meta's VR Glasses. They show no insider buying, routine option monetisation before Connect, a locked-up anchor holder, and no institutional repositioning visible at the 5% or short-register level.
